# Code of conduct

Be kind and be useful. This project is for people of every background and skill level.

- Treat everyone with respect. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests, reviews or any space connected to the project.
- Assume good intent, ask when something is unclear, and say thank you.
- Keep private information (yours and other people's) out of public posts.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that break these rules, and may block people who keep doing it. To report a problem with someone's behaviour, use a private security advisory on this repository or contact a maintainer directly.
