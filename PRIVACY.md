# Privacy

Alpha Assistant has **no server of its own**, no account with us, no analytics and no ads.

**Stays on your phone, encrypted:** your chats, bots, memories, files, routines, and the sign-ins and keys you give the app (encrypted with a key in the Android Keystore).

**Leaves your phone only when it has to for what you asked:**

- Each request to the **AI service you chose** (your ChatGPT plan, an OpenAI key, or another service you entered): the conversation, the files and page text the bot is working with, and any picture it needs to look at.
- Websites a bot **visits** and **connectors** you link (Google, Supabase, Vercel, MCP servers) receive what the bot sends them, just like using those services yourself.
- Texts a bot **sends** go out from your phone number through Android's messaging.
- Downloads you start: the optional voice model and caption model (from Hugging Face), animated emoji (from Google's font servers) and free photos (Wikimedia, Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay).

Read each provider's own privacy terms: they apply to what you send them.

**Your data, your control:** Settings has buttons to erase what a bot learned, to clear browser data, and to sign out. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.

If you report a problem, do not paste secrets, private chats or screenshots with personal information.
