02What to expect

A clear process, from first call to final word.

{{business}} is built around a simple idea: people deserve straight answers and a plan they understand. We take the time to learn your situation before we recommend anything, and we tell you honestly if we are not the right fit.

  1. 01

    Get in touch

    Call, text or send the short form. A few lines about the situation is plenty; there is nothing to prepare.

    A plan you understand

    You get a clear outline of the next steps, the likely timeline and how fees work, so there are no surprises.

  4. 04

    We see it through

    We keep you updated at each stage and stay easy to reach when new questions come up.

04Questions

Good questions to ask before the first meeting

Not covered here? Call {{phone}} and ask. There is no such thing as a small question.

What happens at the first meeting?

We talk through the situation, answer your questions and outline the options. Bring any letters, records or documents you already have; if you are unsure what matters, bring it anyway.

How do fees work?

Explain this in plain words: a fixed fee, an hourly rate, insurance, or a free first conversation. People decide faster when they know what to expect.

How soon can I get an appointment?

Call or send the form and we will offer the next available time. If something is urgent, say so when you get in touch.

Can we meet by phone or video?

Say which kinds of appointment you offer and how they work, so people who cannot travel know they are welcome.

Is what I tell you kept private?

Describe how you handle confidential information in one or two plain sentences, and who to ask for more detail.

What if you are not the right fit?

We will tell you early and, where we can, point you to someone better placed to help.
