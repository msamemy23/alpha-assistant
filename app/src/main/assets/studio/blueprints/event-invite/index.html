02The day

How the day unfolds

Times are a guide. Come as you are and stay as long as you like.

  1. Doors open

    Guests arrive

    Find your seat, say hello and settle in. Describe the welcome here.

  2. +30 min

    The main moment

    The ceremony, the speech, the reveal or the toast: whatever brings everyone together.

  3. +1 h 15

    Food and drinks

    Describe the meal: a seated dinner, a buffet, or drinks and small plates.

  4. +3 h

    Music and dancing

    Say who is playing, or what to expect once the music starts.

  5. Late

    Farewell

    A last dance, a send-off, or simply time to say goodbye.

05Good to know

Questions, answered

Dress code

Say what to wear in one line, such as smart casual or garden party, and mention comfortable shoes if there is grass.

Parking

Explain where to park and whether it costs anything, or suggest the easiest way to arrive without a car.

Gifts

Having you there is the gift. If guests ask, add a kind line here about a registry, a cause or a card box.

Plus-ones and children

Let guests know who the invitation is for, so nobody has to ask.

Food and allergies

Describe what will be served and ask guests to mention any dietary needs in their RSVP.

Access

Note step-free access, seating or quiet spaces, and who to contact for anything else.
